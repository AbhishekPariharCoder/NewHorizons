Shaping Civil Servants Since 2017

New Horizons Tutorials is Bhopal's dedicated coaching center for competitive exam aspirants. Located in the heart of M.P. Nagar Zone 2, we have been guiding students toward MPPSC, UPSC, SSC, Banking, and Vyapam success since 2017.

Our approach combines strong conceptual clarity, regular mock testing, and personalized doubt sessions — ensuring every student is exam-ready with the right strategy.
